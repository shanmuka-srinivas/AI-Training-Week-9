# Neapolitan Pizza Dough

- recipe_id: R-002
- cuisine: italian
- dietary_tags: vegan, dairy-free, contains-gluten
- yield: six 250 g dough balls
- hydration: 62%

## About this dough

A Neapolitan dough is defined more by what is absent than by what is present.
No oil, no sugar, no milk. Flour, water, salt and a very small amount of yeast,
worked hard and then left alone for a long time in the cold, which is where all
of the flavour comes from.

The tiny yeast weight is not a typo. A long cold ferment needs almost no yeast,
and the number in the table is what makes twenty-four hours in the fridge
produce flavour rather than a dough that has blown itself out overnight.

The third column is baker's percentage against the flour weight.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Tipo 00 flour | 900 g | 100% |
| Water (18 C) | 558 g | 62% |
| Fine sea salt | 27 g | 3% |
| Fresh yeast | 1.8 g | 0.2% |
| Total dough | 1486.8 g | 165.2% |

## Method

Dissolve the salt in the water first, then add roughly a tenth of the flour and
whisk to a slurry. Crumble in the yeast, add the remaining flour, and mix until the
dough comes together into a rough mass.

Knead for ten minutes until the dough is smooth and springs back when pressed.
Cover and rest the bulk mass for two hours at room temperature, then divide into
six equal balls and refrigerate for at least twenty-four hours.

Bring the balls back to room temperature for three hours before stretching. Bake in
a wood oven at 450 C for ninety seconds.

## Storage and troubleshooting

The balls hold in the fridge for three days and improve for the first two. Bring
them to room temperature for three hours before stretching or the gluten will
fight you and the base will spring back in the oven.

A base that tears while stretching was under-rested. A tough, bready rim means
too much flour went on the bench. Grey patches on a ball mean it dried out and
needed a lidded box rather than a cloth.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten. Free from dairy, egg, nuts and fish.
