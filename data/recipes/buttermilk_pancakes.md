# Buttermilk Pancakes

- recipe_id: R-003
- cuisine: american
- dietary_tags: vegetarian, contains-dairy, contains-egg, contains-gluten
- yield: twelve 12 cm pancakes
- hydration: not applicable

## About this batter

The lumps are the recipe. A pancake batter beaten smooth has had its gluten
developed, and developed gluten in a pancake means a chewy, flat disc instead of
a tender one. Stop folding while the batter still looks slightly wrong.

Buttermilk is here for acid as much as for flavour. The acid reacts with the
bicarbonate of soda immediately and with the baking powder again in the pan,
which is why the batter rests for a quarter of an hour and then goes straight
onto the griddle rather than waiting around.

The third column is a percentage of the flour weight, in the same style as the
bread cards in this book.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Plain flour | 300 g | 100% |
| Cultured buttermilk | 360 g | 120% |
| Whole egg | 100 g | 33.3% |
| Melted butter | 45 g | 15% |
| Caster sugar | 24 g | 8% |
| Baking powder | 9 g | 3% |
| Bicarbonate of soda | 3 g | 1% |
| Fine sea salt | 4 g | 1.3% |
| Total batter | 845 g | 281.6% |

## Method

Whisk the dry ingredients together in a wide bowl. In a second bowl beat the egg
into the buttermilk, then pour in the melted butter in a thin stream.

Fold the wet mixture into the dry with a spatula and stop while the batter is still
lumpy. Rest it for fifteen minutes so the raising agents can start working.

Cook on a lightly buttered griddle over medium heat, about two minutes per side,
turning once the surface bubbles hold their shape.

## Storage and troubleshooting

The batter does not keep. Cook it all and keep the finished pancakes in a low
oven on a rack rather than stacked on a plate, where the steam softens them.

Pancakes that spread thin were made from a batter that sat too long. Pancakes
that are pale and cakey mean the griddle was too cool. A bitter, soapy note
means the bicarbonate was not evenly whisked through the dry ingredients.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten, cow's milk dairy and hen egg.
